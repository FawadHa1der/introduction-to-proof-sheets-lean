About this site

These are the proof sheets of the course Bewijzen in de Wiskunde (Utrecht University), from impermeable/introduction-to-proof-sheets-lean, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, adapted for Lean 4.34. They follow D. Newstead, An Infinite Descent into Pure Mathematics.

Your proofs are checked by Lean 4.34 running in this browser tab (WebAssembly, via QED64), with Mathlib, Verbose Lean and the Waterproof genre for Verso. The editor is the Waterproof editor. Nothing you type leaves your computer; your work is kept in this browser.

Requirements: a desktop Chrome or Edge (133 or later) or Firefox (134 or later), and about 8 GB of free memory.
